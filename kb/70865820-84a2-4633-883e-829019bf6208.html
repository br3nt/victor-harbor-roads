OC3572026 Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) Consultation Approved

26 Oct 2026 — Council, item 10.1 Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy. Made by the City of Victor Harbor.

Approved public consultation on the preliminary Vision using the draft maps (SMEC- and council-branded) and the SMEC/URPS engagement plan; the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) may make minor map amendments. The maps add a one-way loop and a 30 km/h town centre, which SMEC's August report does not mention. They also show signals at Torrens/Seaview and Torrens/Crozier and an outer freight ring road, which that report raised only as options to consider or explore, without testing them.
