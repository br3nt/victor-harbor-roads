2017 Town Centre Traffic Strategy

The Town Centre Traffic Movement and Car Parking Strategy (Tonkin Consulting, with ARRB/Luxmore for parking, URPS for planning and Austraffic for surveys), endorsed by Council's Strategic Planning and Development Policy Committee on 17 Oct 2017; final December 2017 [S-A08a] [S-A08b]. It is the council's last adopted position on town-centre traffic, so it matters for R2 (how options changed) and R5 (options rejected).

Data in the 2017 work (useful for calibration)

  • Turning-movement counts at 7 intersections on Torrens Street, April 2017 — "11 hour" in the strategy [S-A08a p.9], 7am–7pm (12 hours) on Wed 26 Apr 2017 in the Torrens St review, which notes they were taken in school holidays [S-D19]. Outside the summer peak.
  • Burke Street 1,500–1,650 vehicles/day, 85th-percentile speed 40–45 km/h (rat-running) [S-A08a p.10]; Acraman Street about 1,500 vehicles/day, 85th-percentile 46–49 km/h [S-A08a p.15].
  • Parking: about 2,000 on-street spaces in the study area (Oct 2015); average weekday occupancy across the whole study area 26–29%, peak 40% over Christmas–New Year (area-wide averages; the core blocks of Ocean St and Coral St were over 80% on a typical weekday per the investigations report [S-D21 s7.2]); core central business district (CBD) about 513 on-street spaces, never completely full; Esplanade car park (184 spaces) almost empty on weekdays [S-A08b p.11–12]. The same study notes parking in a few individual streets and zones was at capacity [S-A08b p.11]. Inference: spare parking exists within walking distance of the core, but because the core blocks are over 80% full even on a typical weekday, some circling for parking around Ocean St and Coral St is likely on ordinary days, not only in summer.
  • Torrens Street is a State (Department of Planning, Transport and Infrastructure [DPTI], now Department for Infrastructure and Transport [DIT]) road; Council asked DPTI in 2016 for a collaborative review of it [S-A08a p.5]. Appendix C is a Torrens Street Traffic Management Plan prepared with DPTI.
  • Only nine responses to the 2017 draft [S-A08a p.6].
