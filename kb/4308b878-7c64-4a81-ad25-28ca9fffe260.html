Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) Survey

Step-by-step capture of "Survey: Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy" on Social Pinpoint (R10). Walked to the final step without submitting and without entering personal data; full text in [S-A05], maps in [S-A04a–d]. Brent's own submitted answers are in Brent's Position.

Rating scale: Strongly Support · Support · Neutral/Unsure · Do not support · Strongly Do Not Support. Rating matrices are required; comment boxes optional.

  1. Town Centre (map: town centre). Q1 rating matrix over the five features (30 km/h, shared zones, one-way loop, traffic lights, digital parking signage). Q2: Do you have other ideas about making the town centre easy to get around?
  2. Ring Road (map: ring road). Q3 rate Option 1 and Option 2 independently. Q4: other ideas about the ring road.
  3. Public Transport. Q5 six-row matrix; Q6 other ideas.
  4. Walking and Cycling. Q7 four-row matrix; Q8 other ideas.
  5. Other Comments. Q9 free text.
  6. About You. Q10 connection to Victor Harbor (Resident, Rate payer, Worker, Business, Community or business group, Stakeholder, Visitor, Other); required first name, last name, email, suburb; Submit (not clicked).

Design observations

Inference (our assessment, for Brent's submission):

  • Each feature is presented with its claimed benefit attached (to reduce traffic congestion, to improve traffic flow), so respondents rate the benefit claim, not the measure.
  • No alternatives to choose between for the town centre, no costs, no trade-offs, no evidence shown; you can only rate each feature in isolation.
  • Hard-copy survey available at the Civic Centre; written submissions accepted by email (localgov@victor.sa.gov.au), post (PO Box 11, Victor Harbor South Australia 5211) or in person [S-A03 p.8].
