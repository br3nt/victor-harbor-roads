# Assumptions — S1-network

Each entry: value, reason, expected sensitivity, and what evidence would replace it. Sourced values are not listed here; they carry their source id in the data files.

Sourced inputs, for reference: OSM extract S-E01 (roads, tags, roundabouts, turn restrictions);
DIT legal speed limits S-D05; the right turn Torrens St → Ocean St from the 2017 Torrens Street
review S-D19 (fix F1). Validation and results: `sim/data/networks/VALIDATION.md`.
