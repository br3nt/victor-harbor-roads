Assumptions — S1-network

Each entry: value, reason, expected sensitivity, and what evidence would replace it. Sourced values are not listed here; they carry their source id in the data files.

Sourced inputs, for reference: OSM extract S-E01 (roads, tags, roundabouts, turn restrictions); DIT legal speed limits S-D05; the right turn Torrens St → Ocean St from the 2017 Torrens Street review S-D19 (fix F1). Validation and results: sim/data/networks/VALIDATION.md.

Id Assumption Value Reason Sensitivity Would be replaced by
A-S1-01 Speed where neither DIT nor OSM gives one 50 km/h SA default urban limit (Road Traffic Act default in built-up areas) Low in town (council streets, mostly 50 anyway). High on rural roads at the fringe (Greenhills Rd, Cartwright Rd, Waterport Rd east, Wilson Hill Rd, Tugwell Rd): likely 80–100 in reality, so travel times to the north-west/north-east fringes are too long Council speed-zone register; DIT sign layer for council roads; a site check
A-S1-02 Service roads (car-park entries, lanes, access roads) open to all motor vehicles, 20 km/h, 1 lane SUMO's default opens service roads to delivery vehicles only, which would cut car parks off; 20 km/h is SUMO's service-road default Low for network results; moderate for trips that end in car parks Car-park and lane survey
A-S1-03 OSM service ways dropped before import service=driveway|parking_aisle|drive-through|emergency_access, or access=private|no (153 ways) Not public road; car-park aisles would add hundreds of tiny edges and right-before-left junctions with no data on their use Low; destinations still map to the nearest public edge Parking model (if parking search is ever modelled)
A-S1-04 Lanes where OSM has no lanes tag 1 per direction, all classes (SUMO's typemap gives 2 per direction on trunk/primary) Every OSM lane tag on the town's arterials says one per direction; the 2017 review's typical cross-section (a proposal) labels a 1.4 m bike lane, a 3.3 m and a 2.5 m lane, read from the text extraction only [S-D19 fig. 6.1]; no 4-lane road is known in Victor Harbor High if wrong on Hindmarsh Rd / Adelaide Rd (capacity halves or doubles); not checked against imagery Aerial imagery check of each arterial; DIT asset data
A-S1-05 Roundabout circulating speed V = √(127 R (e+f)) km/h with e+f = 0.3, R = mean distance of the ring's SUMO junctions from their centre; capped at the legal limit (gives 18–20 km/h on the Torrens St roundabouts, 27 at McCracken) SUMO does not slow vehicles on curved normal edges; Austroads' speed–radius relation for low-speed curves Moderate for roundabout delay and capacity (gap acceptance, travel time through the ring) Measured circulating speeds; calibration against the 2017 turning counts
A-S1-06 Signalised pedestrian crossings (3) not modelled (signals dropped) Pedestrians are not simulated; a signal with no pedestrian demand would only add random stops Low–moderate on Torrens St near Coral St in busy periods Pedestrian counts and signal timings, if pedestrian delay matters
A-S1-07 Gateway, suburb and town-centre points used for connectivity checks coordinates in vhsim/network/places.py Read off the network (one residential street per suburb; Coral St for the centre; the extract's boundary crossings) None on results (checks only); S2 owns the real zones and gateways S2 zones / gateways
A-S1-08 U-turns where a two-way road splits into one-way carriageways just before the extract ends (3: Victor Harbor Rd, Waterport Rd, Adelaide Rd near the north edge) allowed Stand-in for turning opportunities beyond the extract, so the two-way road stays usable both ways inside the model Low (only trips starting/ending on those stubs) A larger extract (S-S1-01) removes most of them
A-S1-09 Right-turn storage at Torrens St → Ocean St (fix F1) median link of ~10 m; the real ~50 m turn lane [S-D19] not modelled The turn lane is not in OSM and its exact geometry is not archived Moderate: right-turners queue back into the through lane sooner than in reality Concept/as-built plan or imagery; add a turn bay patch
A-S1-10 Turn bays, flared entries and right-turn lanes elsewhere none (OSM has none) No data Moderate–high for delays at priority junctions on Hindmarsh Rd / Torrens St (right-turners block the single lane) Imagery / intersection plans; patches per junction
A-S1-11 Priority at uncontrolled junctions from road class (OSM highway type); right-before-left only where all approaches are below 49 km/h (service roads) Give-way/stop signs on council roads are not in OSM; the class rule matches the T-junction rule at most town junctions Moderate at specific junctions where signs differ Council sign inventory
A-S1-12 DIT spatial match ≥ 60% of the edge within 20 m of the section and parallel within 30°; name in the same DIT road Divided carriageways sit up to ~15 m off the centreline; name check stops side streets taking the State limit Low (checked section by section in VALIDATION.md) —
