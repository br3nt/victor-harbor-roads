Baseline network — validation (S1)

Build: cd sim && python -m vhsim.network.build (≈5 s). Inputs: OSM extract S-E01 (sources/E/S-E01-overpass-victor-harbor.osm, data timestamp 2026-11-20) and DIT State Maintained Roads legal speed limits S-D05 (sources/D/D-state-maintained-roads-vh-area.geojson). Every check below is an automated test in sim/tests/network/ (fast: -m "not slow"; the slow tests build the real network). Counts and lists are in build_report.json. Choices not taken from a source are in sim/assumptions/S1-network.md (ids A-S1-xx).

Import (requirement 1)

netconvert 1.28 options (vhsim/network/config.py): --lefthand, --geometry.remove, --roundabouts.guess, --junctions.join, --tls.guess false, --tls.guess-signals false, --tls.discard-loaded, --osm.turn-lanes, --output.street-names, --output.original-names, --keep-edges.by-vclass passenger,truck,trailer,delivery,bus, --remove-edges.isolated, --osm.all-attributes (OSM highway, maxspeed, lanes, oneway, ref, junction, service kept as edge params). SUMO's OSM typemap is loaded with vhsim/network/vh.typ.xml on top (A-S1-01/02/04).

  • Dropped: footways, cycleways, paths, steps, pedestrian ways, tracks, construction/proposed ways (no motor-vehicle class; tested). Before import, 153 OSM service ways that are not public road are removed (A-S1-03): 66 access=private, 1 access=no (a bus-only link), 48 service=driveway, 38 service=parking_aisle. Other service roads (car-park entries, lanes, access roads; 777 edges) are kept and open to cars at 20 km/h (A-S1-02).
  • Traffic lights: none. OSM has no signalised road junctions; its only three signal nodes are pedestrian crossings (Torrens St near Coral St ×2, near Bay Rd / Franklin Pde) [S-E01]. They are dropped with --tls.discard-loaded; pedestrians are not modelled, so a signal that only serves a crossing would only add random stops (A-S1-06). The DIT signal layer also has none [S-D10].
  • Cut at the extract edge: 1 edge removed (an unnamed one-way service stub entering from nowhere, near the hospital). Every other edge is drivable for cars (next section).
  • netconvert warnings (counts in build_report.json): 23 "Replacing loaded roundabout": the first segment of a closed OSM roundabout way is merged into its neighbour by --geometry.remove, so the roundabout is re-registered without that (no longer existing) edge id. This is what research/E-network.md called "~20 incomplete roundabouts" (with its options the same message read "Writing incomplete roundabout ... no longer exist"); it does not leave any ring incomplete (next check). 33 sharp-turn and 48 turning-radius speed reductions (geometry); 24 "intersecting left turns" at joined clusters, mostly Encounter Bay residential streets; 1 ignored restriction (re-applied, see turn restrictions).

Speeds (requirement 2)

Rule: DIT legal limit [S-D05] where the edge runs along a State road section; else OSM maxspeed [S-E01]; else 50 km/h (20 km/h on service roads). An edge takes a DIT section when ≥ 60% of points sampled every 5 m along it lie within 20 m of the section centreline and run parallel to it (within 30°), and its name belongs to the same DIT road number (or it is an unnamed trunk/primary/secondary/link piece). Each edge's source is in edge_attributes.csv (speed_source, dit_section, osm_maxspeed, legal_speed_kmh).

DIT warns its legal limits "may not coincide with the posted speed limit signage" [S-D06]. The DIT regulatory sign extract [S-D11] supports the DIT value in the town: a 50 sign on Torrens St near Crozier Rd (SI-105902, -35.5549, 138.6204), a 50 sign on Hindmarsh Rd before Torrens St (SI-105466, -35.5511, 138.6235), back-to-back 50/60 signs where Hindmarsh Rd changes (SI-105373/105374 and SI-105376/105377, about -35.5500, 138.6241, i.e. RRD ≈ 49.5), a 50 sign on Victoria St near King St (SI-105967) and back-to-back 50/60 signs at Victoria St / Bay Rd (SI-111221/111233). So OSM's 60 on Torrens St and Victoria St looks out of date. Tested: test_torrens_street_uses_dit_50_not_osm_60, test_state_roads_take_dit_limits.

Roundabouts: circulating edges get V = √(127 R (e+f)), e+f = 0.3, R = ring radius from the SUMO junction positions (A-S1-05), never above the legal limit: 18–20 km/h at the Torrens St roundabouts, 23 at Bay Rd, 27 at McCracken, 31 at Armstrong Rd / George Main Rd (tested 15–35).

Defaults that are probably too low (outside the town): untagged rural roads default to 50 km/h: Greenhills Rd 17.1 km (directed), Cartwright Rd 9.0, Waterport Rd 7.8 (its tagged part is 80), Wilson Hill Rd 5.2, Tugwell Rd 4.9, Jagger Rd 4.4 km. They only affect travel times to/from the north-west and north-east fringes. OSM tags Waitpinga Rd 110 km/h (22 km); unverified (SA's open-road default is 100).

Where OSM tags lanes on these roads it always says one per direction (e.g. lanes=2 on two-way Hindmarsh Rd and Adelaide Rd, lanes=1 on the divided Torrens St and McCracken approaches). Not checked against aerial imagery (no imagery source is archived; this session had none). Turn pockets, flared roundabout entries and right-turn bays are not in OSM and not modelled (A-S1-10): a right-turner waiting on a one-lane approach blocks through traffic. This matters most for right turns from Torrens St / Hindmarsh Rd into Stuart St, Cornhill Rd and Grantley Ave, and for the ~50 m right-turn lane into Ocean St that the 2017 review describes [S-D19 s3]; add them as patches once the layout is checked.

Fix F1 — Torrens St → Ocean St right turn. OSM has no median opening at Torrens St / Ocean St, so the right turn from the north-east-bound carriageway into Ocean St did not exist. The 2017 review counts about 700 such right turns a day and recommends retaining them [S-D19 s3]. The build adds a one-lane median link (vhsim/network/fixes/, edge vhfix_torrens_ocean_right) that yields to the south-west-bound carriageway and leads only into Ocean St (tested). The real ~50 m storage is not modelled (A-S1-09). The other Torrens St side-street turns (Coral, Hill, Stuart: left and right) are present.

Roundabouts (requirement 4)

Connectivity (requirement 5)

  • Strongly connected: every edge is usable by cars, trucks and caravans (trailer): the network is the largest strongly connected car component plus the one-way chains that link it to the extract boundary (checks.drivable_edges; tested for passenger, truck, trailer).
  • Boundary U-turns (A-S1-08): where a two-way road splits into one-way carriageways just before the extract ends (Victor Harbor Rd / Waterport Rd junction), netconvert builds no turnaround, so the two-way road beyond the split was exit-only one way and entry-only the other. Three U-turns are added (Victor Harbor Rd at -35.5221, Waterport Rd at -35.5201, Adelaide Rd at -35.5266). After that, only edges touching the boundary itself lie outside the inner component (tested), so any origin/destination mapped by lon/lat can reach any other inside the model.
  • Gateways (gateway_edges.csv): Victor Harbor Rd north, Port Elliot Rd east, Inman Valley Rd west, Waitpinga Rd south-west, plus Welch Rd north, Greenhills Rd north and Waterport Rd east. duarouter routes cars, trucks and caravans from every gateway to the town centre (Coral St) and back, and trucks between every pair of gateways (tested).
  • Hindmarsh Tiers Rd is not in the extract. It meets Victor Harbor Rd at about -35.4986, 138.6289 [S-D05], north of S-E01's bbox (north edge -35.52); its traffic must enter on the Victor Harbor Rd north gateway (hindmarsh_tiers_rd row resolves to the same edges).
  • The ring road is cut. S-E01 ends on Welch Rd at -35.5199, 138.6231, before Welch Rd meets Victor Harbor Rd. In the model, Victor Harbor Rd north and Welch Rd north are separate gateways; traffic that uses the ring road between Victor Harbor Rd and Inman Valley Rd / Waitpinga Rd must be injected at the Welch Rd gateway, or it will route through town. The northern extension S-S1-01 (below) closes this.
  • Suburbs: a residential street in each of Victor Harbor, Encounter Bay, Hayborough, McCracken and Hindmarsh Valley (its part inside the extract, on Waterport Rd) routes to and from the town centre by fromLonLat/toLonLat (the S2 trip format; tested).

Turn restrictions

All 16 OSM restriction relations map onto net movements (vhsim/network/restrictions.py; tested: none violated). Five had been lost and are re-applied as connection deletions in the plain files:

  • Victoria St 17070777–80 (at Burke, King, Granite, Island Sts). OSM tags them no_right_turn into the median opening from one carriageway; the opening only leads to the opposite carriageway, so in effect they ban U-turns through the median. --junctions.join merged both carriageways and the opening into one junction, the restriction was lost and a U-turn appeared; the build deletes it. Correction for the knowledge base: these are not bans on turning right from Victoria St into the side streets. Per OSM, eastbound traffic may still turn right into King St and Burke St across the median, and westbound into Granite St and Island St.
  • Torrens St 8181749 (no U-turn via the Torrens Lane median link, a via-way relation netconvert ignored): re-applied.

Named junctions (requirement 6)

junctions.csv: 37 intersections from research/E-network.md table E20b plus four on the ring road. Each is located by the streets that meet there near its OSM position; control is read back from the network and tested against the OSM control type (control_osm); sumo_nodes, in_edges and out_edges give the SUMO elements for movement metrics (S4). All 37 found; all match.

Notes: Torrens St / Coral St has 3 legs (Coral St runs east only; the short trunk_link across the median is inside the joined junction). Hindmarsh Rd / Grantley Ave / Bridge Tce includes the Bridge Tce node 32 m away and service accesses (6 legs). Where two streets of equal class meet (e.g. Coral St / Ocean St) both are listed as major_road.

Scenario patches (requirement 7)

vhsim.network.scenario.build(name, patches_dir) loads the baseline plain files plus *.nod|edg|con|tll|typ.xml patches and writes sim/data/networks/<name>.net.xml. The baseline net itself is built the same way with no patches. Test scenario patches/test_torrens_40/ sets one Torrens St edge (50904910#0) to 40 km/h; the test asserts that edge, and only that edge, changes. The plain files are deterministic (two builds are identical).

Smoke simulation (requirement 8)

python -m vhsim.network.smoke (random trips, 10% trucks, one hour, --time-to-teleport 300):

Run Trips Arrived Teleports (jam / yield) Jam teleports at roundabouts
random, seed 42 400 400 0 0
random, seed 1 400 400 0 0
random, seed 1 3,000 3,000 0 0
town-focused (--mode town), seed 7 1,500 1,500 0 0
town-focused, seeds 7 and 8 3,000 3,000 0 0
town-focused, seed 7 (overload) 6,000 6,000 92 (30 / 62) 0

--mode town sends half the trips within 1.2 km of the town centre and half between gateways and the centre. At 3,000 trips/h the Torrens St roundabouts take ~200–550 entering vehicles/h (below the 2017 peak-hour counts, e.g. 1,823/h at Torrens/Seaview/Eyre [S-D19]). At 6,000/h minor-street entries onto Hindmarsh Rd and Acraman St start timing out (yield teleports), but no roundabout deadlocks. Gridlock at roundabouts appears only far beyond observed demand (9,000/h). Random trips are not a demand model; capacity is checked again once S2/S3 demand and calibration exist.

Northern extension S-S1-01 (pending download)

The northern growth areas (UGMS areas 2 and 3, north of area 1) reach about -35.48, north of S-E01. python -m vhsim.network.fetch_osm fetches a supplementary Overpass extract with S-E01's tag filters (query sources/E/query1.overpassql, bbox -35.60, 138.55, -35.46, 138.67) into sim/data/networks/raw/S-S1-01-overpass-victor-harbor-north.osm, saves the query as raw/S-S1-01.overpassql and writes raw/SOURCES.tsv (id, url, query, OSM data timestamp, retrieved_at, sha256). When that file exists, python -m vhsim.network.build uses it instead of S-E01 (its bbox contains S-E01's), with the north edge at -35.46 for fringe detection and its own gateway list (Victor Harbor Rd and Hindmarsh Tiers Rd at the DIT centreline crossings of -35.46). Tests waiting for it (skipped until then): every northern road (Hutchinson Rd, Hindmarsh Tiers Rd, Waggon Rd, Day Rd, Greenhills Rd, Victor Harbor Rd, Waterport Rd, Welch Rd) routes to and from the town centre, Hindmarsh Tiers Rd is its own gateway, and a truck from Victor Harbor Rd north to Inman Valley Rd can use Welch Rd / Armstrong Rd. Not yet fetched: downloading needs the user's approval. Everything above describes the S-E01 build; rerun the build and this file's numbers after the fetch.

Still approximate

  1. Lanes and turn bays: not imagery-checked; no turn pockets or flared entries (see Lanes).
  2. Ring road north end and Hindmarsh Tiers Rd outside the extract (see Connectivity).
  3. Junction geometry comes from OSM node positions and --junctions.join; divided-road junctions are single SUMO junctions; internal turning speeds are netconvert's radius estimates.
  4. Rural untagged roads at 50 km/h; Waitpinga Rd 110 km/h from OSM (see Speeds).
  5. Give-way/stop signs are not imported: priority comes from road class (the T-junction rule). The only OSM stop/give_way nodes are at Cockle Train level crossings; trains and the 23 railway crossings are not modelled.
  6. Car parks: car-park aisles are dropped; entries are dead-end service stubs. No parking search or parking capacity.
  7. Residential roundabouts outside the town centre: complete but small, OSM-shaped (table above).
  8. Pedestrian crossings (3 signalised, others zebra) and pedestrians are not modelled.
