Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy

How would the road options change your trips?

An independent traffic simulation of Victor Harbor, built by a resident. It compares today's roads with the Council's proposed town-centre changes and with alternatives, on a school-term weekday and a summer holiday day, now and as the town grows (about 5 years ahead, and further out).

Your trips

Add the trips you make most often and see whether each option makes them faster, slower or about the same. Your trips stay in your own browser.

Detailed results viewer

Congestion lane by lane and direction by direction, travel time to any place, a timeline through the day, and before/after comparisons for every simulated option.

Evidence and method

The council decisions, documents, data sources, assumptions, calibration and full results behind the simulation, each linked to its source.

Results are preliminary and best read as differences between options on the same traffic, not exact forecasts. The model is partly calibrated against Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) counts.
