Victor Harbor's road changes: what the evidence shows

A traffic simulation of the whole town, run on the council's proposed changes and on cheaper alternatives.

Brent Jacobs, Encounter Bay resident and ratepayer, writing independently. "Now" is built on 2025 data. Use the arrow keys, tap or swipe.

The proposal

The council's town-centre package

  • 30 km/h and shared streets in the town centre
  • A one-way loop: Torrens St, Eyre Tce, Flinders Pde, Esplanade
  • Traffic signals at Torrens St / Seaview Rd and Torrens St / Crozier Rd

I simulated it against today's roads, with today's traffic.

3 · Never tested or costed

No report tests the loop or 30 km/h

  • The consultant SMEC's only report (Aug 2026) contains neither; only a "Do Nothing" network was run.
  • Testing, modelling and costings: Feb–Jun 2027, after this consultation.
  • Council debate on 26 Oct 2026: about 5 minutes; nobody mentioned the loop, 30 km/h or the signals. No costs.
  • Newest town-centre counts: April 2017.

3 · The State's own guide

Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) Speed Limit Guideline

A 30/40 km/h limit "is not intended for streets with short holiday or tourism peaks"

Freight and major traffic routes are "unsuitable". Torrens St and Hindmarsh Rd are State roads and freight routes.

5 · Ring road and 6 · Growth

Ring road

Widening today's ring to four lanes alone makes 2041 worse unless the Crozier Rd junction is fixed (wait about 29 s → 50–55 s).

Option 1 (new outer ring road, old ring at 60 km/h): +18–30% queueing in 2041, and about 28% more trucks in the town centre. Option 2 + junction fixes: −24%.

Growth

4×

today's time stopped, term weekday, 2041 high growth. Summer jams in every option. The whole network, with its future streets, must be modelled before committing.

7 · What we ask Council

Before endorsing a Vision

  • Publish the model and the data
  • Model every option, including summer and event days, against both growth scenarios
  • Collect current traffic counts
  • Trial before committing, with before/after data
  • Put alternatives on the table: turn lanes, roundabouts, time-based speed limits, two-way streets
  • Publish costs, and answer the questions
