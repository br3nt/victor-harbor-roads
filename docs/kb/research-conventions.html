Research Conventions

How provenance is kept (R18). The working copy for research agents is Research conventions (provenance rules) in the project.

  • Every source gets an id (S-<stream><nn>), an archived copy in sources/<stream>/, its retrieval time and a sha256 hash, recorded in the Sources Register.
  • Every claim cites its source id, with a page, section, or short quote.
  • What a source says is kept apart from our inference; inferences are labelled.
  • Numbers keep their units, date and geography as the source states them. Disagreeing sources are both recorded.
  • Primary sources (council documents and minutes, state data, Australian Bureau of Statistics [ABS]) outrank news and social media, which are leads only.
  • In notes, a source citation is a link to its row in the Sources Register (#S-xxx); an external link carries rel="external".
  • Status attributes (data-status="todo|partial|found|missing") on requirements and expected items make progress machine-readable.
