Questions for Council

Questions the public record doesn't answer, collected as research finds them. Feeds Brent's submission (R24) and, where needed, a Freedom of Information (FOI) request under the Freedom of Information Act 1991 (South Australia [SA]). Contact: localgov@victor.sa.gov.au, (08) 8551 0500.

Ask directly

  1. Which streets form the one-way outer loop, in which direction, and what happens to Torrens Street two-way traffic? (Map only, Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy [VHITS])
  2. Exact boundary of the 30 km/h area; locations and hours of shared use zones.
  3. What traffic modelling (e.g. SIDRA Intersection, microsimulation) shows the loop, the signals and 30 km/h will reduce traffic congestion / improve traffic flow? Under which conditions (summer peak, events)?
  4. The 2017 strategy recommended a roundabout (since built) at Torrens/Crozier and said signals at Hindmarsh/Seaview/Eyre needed further detailed investigations and modelling; the 2015–16 investigations found signals there not justified at that time but possibly required if more land is developed. What modelling now supports replacing both roundabouts with signals? (2017 strategy)
  5. Who prepared the preliminary Vision (staff or consultant), and what demographic and transport data did it use?
  6. Has the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) been consulted on, or agreed to, signals and speed changes on Torrens Street / Hindmarsh Road (a State road)?
  7. What base year and figure does the "double to about 36,000 by 2051" forecast start from?
  8. Costs, funding sources and staging for each option; the ring road options in particular.
  9. Which Council meeting will consider the consultation results, and when will the Strategy be adopted?
  10. The Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) Speed Limit Guideline (version 3, 2023) allows a 30 km/h or 40 km/h limit in town centres [S-M04 s5.7]. It also says the high-pedestrian-activity limit is not intended for streets with short holiday or tourism peaks, that freight routes or major traffic routes are unsuitable for it, and that 30 km/h is best suited to locations where the road has been specifically designed to create a speed environment of 30 km/h and alternative routes are available to drivers [S-M04 s5.7.1]. Torrens St and Hindmarsh Rd are primary freight routes [S-D08]. Which streets would the 30 km/h limit cover, how does council say they meet those tests, and has DIT indicated it would approve 30 km/h there? (Approvals)
  11. The 2015–16 study said signals at Torrens/Seaview/Eyre need three lanes per approach and land acquisition. Which land, and at what cost?
  12. The newest town-centre turning counts appear to be from April 2017. Has council counted traffic since, especially in summer?
  13. What happened to the 2017 Torrens Street Traffic Management Review recommendations (Grantley Ave half-closure, Stuart St right-turn ban, median)?
  14. Were alternatives considered (e.g. roundabout upgrades, slip lanes, two-lane roundabouts, widening) and why were they not offered in the survey?

Added from the decision record

  1. Who proposed the one-way loop and the 30 km/h town centre? Neither is in SMEC's August report, but both are on the SMEC- and council-branded maps. What evidence supports each, and what was presented at the briefings and the 30 Sep 2026 staff workshop?
  2. SMEC named the existing ring road the preferred freight route and said a northern bypass "should be explored". What exploration made the outer freight ring road part of the Vision? Are there route options, costs, or Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) or heavy-vehicle-regulator advice?
  3. Why consult on options before the Feb–Jun 2027 testing and costing? Will the results be published before the "Ultimate Vision" is endorsed in Feb 2027?
  4. SMEC's daily volumes (Hindmarsh Rd 9,500, Torrens St 8,500) are roughly 1.5–2 times lower than the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT)'s (17,300–19,300; 12,900–15,500), and below SMEC's own 15,000/day definition of an arterial road. Which does the model use?
  5. Phase 1 cost; why $100,000 (contract) differs from $120,000 (budget); why "Project #Planning (100087)" (labelled "C&D Admin" elsewhere) funds it.
  6. Will holiday-period counts and parking surveys be collected before Feb 2027, as SMEC recommends?
  7. Was item 10.1 debated on 26 Oct 2026? (meeting recording)
  8. The Urban Growth Management Strategy (UGMS) plans for two scenarios (≈ +8,000 people on trend; +19,200 under Greater Adelaide Regional Plan [GARP] high growth), and building since 2021 is below even the trend rate. SMEC's report gives both and explains it models the high case as an "ultimate" scenario for consistency with the State plan, but the fact sheet presents only "approximately double … about 36,000 by 2051". Will the public material show both, and will the options be tested against the moderate one?
  9. What is the measured summer peak population and traffic? "Doubles", "30,000+" and "trebles" all appear in council material without a stated source.
