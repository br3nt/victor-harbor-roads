Road Network

The town's road network as OpenStreetMap records it (data timestamp 20 Nov 2026), the base for the simulation network (R12). Extract and analysis: [S-E01]; full write-up Stream E: road network, points of interest, residential areas, SUMO feasibility.

The arterial spine

Torrens Street, Victoria Street, Bay Road, Hindmarsh Road and Port Elliot Road carry route B37; Adelaide Road and Victor Harbor Road carry A13 — State-numbered routes, tagged trunk/primary at 60 km/h. Torrens and Victoria Streets are divided roads (split carriageways). Ocean Street and Railway Terrace are already one-way [S-E01].

Key town-centre intersections today

No intersection in the area is signalised. The only signals are three pedestrian crossings (two on Torrens Street near Coral Street, one near Bay Road/Franklin Parade) [S-E01]. To be confirmed on the ground.

Data quality for simulation

  • Of 1,323 drivable ways: lanes missing on 95%, speed limit on 60%. Arterial segments without a speed tag would default to the wrong speed in Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO), so lanes and speeds on the arterials must be set by hand from imagery and the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) speed-zone data.
  • About 20 roundabouts import as "incomplete" in Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO) and need checking by hand.
  • Car-park capacities are almost entirely missing (146 car parks, 2 with a capacity).
  • Granite Island causeway is pedestrian / horse tram only — not a car route.

Points of interest

405 POIs extracted [S-E03]: 12 supermarket/food retail (Woolworths at Victor Central, Drakes, IGA; Coles and Aldi at McCracken), 33 other shops, 17 food and drink, 1 hospital, 2 pharmacies, 6 fuel, 4 caravan/camp sites, 5 hotels/motels, 2 schools, 64 sports pitches, 2 golf courses, 14 beaches, 24 bus stops. Cafes, restaurants, clinics and gyms are thinly mapped — supplement before computing accessibility (R14).

Residential areas

71 residential land-use areas (874.5 ha); 11 construction areas (29.5 ha), including new streets at Seascape Rise and Sandpiper Drive, Encounter Bay. Suburbs with boundaries: Victor Harbor, Encounter Bay, Hayborough, McCracken, Hindmarsh Valley, Port Elliot, Chiton, Back Valley, Lower Inman Valley, Waitpinga [S-E02].
