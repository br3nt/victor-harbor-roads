SMEC Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) Phase 1 Report

Author: SMEC.

Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy, SMEC with URPS, Revision 2, 21 Aug 2026 (82 pp; Rev 1 30 Jun 2026). Tabled as an attachment on 26 Oct 2026 labelled "Draft Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy" [S-B04] [S-B03 p.80].

  • Contains no one-way loop and no 30 km/h figure (searched: "one-way", "loop" other than bus loop services, "30 km/h").
  • Raises, as options to consider (none tested): Review of speed limits in high pedestrian areas and the possible adoption of lower speeds or shared traffic zones in areas such as the central business district (CBD) [S-B04 p.70]; Signalisation of intersections for safer pedestrian crossings [S-B04 p.50]; on Torrens St the critical junctions with Seaview Road and Crozier Road [S-B04 p.52]; and Consideration of signalising roundabouts and the installation of traffic signals as key east-west intersections reach capacity [S-B04 p.71].
  • Names Armstrong Road (the existing ring road) the preferred freight route [S-B04 p.50], and also says An alternative scenario for the movement of freight is to provide a new freight bypass further north whose benefits should be explored [S-B04 pp.66, 74-75].
  • Model, data and limits: see Due Diligence Assessment.
  • Refers to a separate "Background Investigations Report" (June 2026) that is not public [S-B04 p.16].
