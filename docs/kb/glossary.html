Glossary

Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT)
Annual Average Daily Traffic: vehicles per day on a road, averaged over a year. Hides seasonal peaks, which matter a lot in a holiday town.
Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT)
The South Australian Government department that controls state roads and approves speed limits and traffic control devices.
Estimated Resident Population (ERP)
The Australian Bureau of Statistics' official population estimate for an area, updated yearly between censuses.
GEH statistic
A measure used to compare modelled and observed traffic volumes; under 5 is a good match.
Level of Service (LoS)
A–F grade for how well an intersection or road performs, based on delay.
Mesh block / Statistical Area Level 1 (SA1) / Statistical Area Level 2 (SA2)
Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) geographic units from smallest (a few dozen dwellings) up to suburb-sized. Used to place households on the map.
Microsimulation
Traffic model that moves every vehicle individually (e.g. Simulation of Urban MObility [SUMO], VISSIM, Aimsun). Contrast with SIDRA Intersection, which analyses one intersection at a time.
p95 / p99
95th / 99th percentile: the time that 95% / 99% of trips beat. Captures the bad days that averages hide.
Ring road
In Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS): a route around the town for through traffic. Option 1 is a new outer ring road; Option 2 retains the current ring road.
Shared use zone
A street where pedestrians have priority over vehicles; Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) proposes 10–20 km/h.
SIDRA Intersection
Industry-standard software for analysing a single intersection's capacity and delay.
Slip lane
A separate turning lane that bypasses the main intersection (e.g. a left-turn bypass at a roundabout).
Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO)
Open-source traffic microsimulator we plan to use.
Turning movement count
A count of vehicles making each movement (left/through/right) from each approach of an intersection over a period.
Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS)
Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy, the council strategy under consultation.
