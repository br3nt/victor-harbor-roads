New options to find (R16)

Generated once the calibrated baseline shows where the delay actually is. Candidates to screen, none endorsed yet:

  • N1 Close (or half-close) the Grantley Avenue rail crossing to relieve the Seaview/Eyre roundabout (from the 2015–16 study, D14).
  • Targeted turn restrictions or left-in/left-out at the worst-performing approaches.
  • Roundabout metering signals that run only in holiday peaks.
  • Signed caravan / heavy-vehicle route that keeps them off the foreshore loop.
  • Seasonal traffic management plans for known peak days (New Year's Eve, Schoolies, Easter).
  • Combinations: e.g. B3 + B5 + B2.
