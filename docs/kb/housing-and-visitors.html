Housing and Visitors

Who lives in each area, how many homes stand empty, and how many visitors arrive (R7, R9). Census 2021 figures are from QuickStats [S-C01] unless stated.

Households

  • 9,746 private dwellings: 7,156 occupied (76.2%), 2,245 unoccupied (23.9%; South Australia [SA] 10.8%). Down from 29.1% in 2016 [S-C15].
  • 2.1 people per occupied household (South Australia [SA] 2.4); 33% lone-person households.
  • 88% separate houses; 3 bedrooms typical (average 3.1).
  • 1.7 vehicles per occupied dwelling: none 5.4%, one 44.5%, two 34.2%, three or more 14.7%.
  • Journey to work: car driver 62.8%, passenger 5.1%, public transport 0.7%, walked only 3.1%, worked at home 11.3%.

Holiday homes, short stays and visitors

  • About 40% of ratepayers live outside the council area [S-C25]. Most holiday dwellings are occupied over summer [S-C14 s.4.3.4].
  • Short-term rental: about 210 Airbnb and 379 Stayz listings (April 2023) [S-C14 s.4.5]. The report calls this "up to 5.1% of unoccupied dwellings", which doesn't reconcile (589 is ≈ 26% of 2,245; listings may overlap).
  • Peak population: council says the population doubles in summer and the town attracts about 1.2 million visitors a year [S-C25]; the Long Term Financial Plan reportedly says 30,000+ (not archived); a .id article says it trebles [S-C06]. No measured peak population exists.
  • Fleurieu region: 893,000 domestic overnight visitors and 2.6 million day trips (year to Dec 2021) [S-C15 s.5.9].

Inference for the model: filling every empty dwelling at 2.1 people adds only ≈ 4,700 people (+29%). A doubling needs caravan parks, short-term lets, friends and relatives and day-trippers as well, so summer is modelled as a separate visitor demand layer (arriving via Victor Harbor Rd and Port Elliot Rd, staying near the coast, heading to the foreshore and centre) rather than by scaling residents' trips. The one measured anchor is the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT)'s >11,000 vehicles/day on Victor Harbor Rd in the summer peak (Traffic and Crash Data).
