Discoveries Log

Things found that the Expected Information Catalogue didn't anticipate, newest first. Each says what it changed in the Investigation Plan.

  1. 21 Nov 2026 — Five documents retrieved through Brent's Chrome: the council's Public Road Register (confirms the town-centre streets are council roads; Torrens St and Victoria St are State) [S-M01]; the State's population projections (the council's 25,292 for 2041 is the State high series, and the population is tracking below the State's medium series) [S-M02]; the 2026/27 event calendar with expected attendance (New Year's Eve 8,000, not the 5,000 we assumed) [S-M03]; the primary speed-limit guideline (see D10) [S-M04]; and the auto-caption transcript of the 26 Oct 2026 debate on item 10.1, about five minutes, with no mention of the loop, 30 km/h, signals or the ring road [S-M05]. Plan change: Approvals and Constraints, Population and Growth, Seasons and Events, People and Roles and Decision Timeline updated; the retrieval list is shorter (Manual Retrieval List).
  2. 21 Nov 2026 — Schoolies Festival runs 20–22 Nov 2026 at Warland Reserve, on the foreshore side of the proposed loop, with road closures [S-C32]. Plan change: event conditions in the simulation use the published closures; a chance to observe event traffic this weekend.
  3. 21 Nov 2026 — Filling every empty dwelling adds only ≈ 4,700 people (+29%), so the claimed summer "doubling" must come mostly from holiday parks, short stays and day visitors [S-C01] [S-C25]. Plan change: summer is a separate visitor demand layer in the model.
  4. 21 Nov 2026 — Median age is 60 and 42% are 65+ [S-C01]. Plan change: trip timing and purposes in the model follow a retiree-heavy profile (fits the midday peak, D12).
  5. 21 Nov 2026 — The council's own Urban Growth Management Strategy (UGMS) gives two scenarios: ≈ +8,000 people by 2050 on trend, or > +19,200 under the State's high-growth targets; actual building (159 dwellings/yr since 2021) is below even the trend rate. The Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) "double to ~36,000" is the high scenario only [S-B10 pp.13, 20, 30, 41]. Plan change: simulate both scenarios; question added for council.
  6. 21 Nov 2026 — SMEC's AADTs (Hindmarsh Rd 9,500, Torrens St 8,500) are roughly 1.5–2 times lower than the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT)'s (17,300–19,300; 12,900–15,500) [S-B04 p.22] [S-D01]. Plan change: calibrate to DIT and the 2017 turning counts; question added for council.
  7. 21 Nov 2026 — The engagement plan scheduled the fact sheet and survey to be uploaded to Your Say by 23 Oct, with public launch on 29 Oct after the 26 Oct vote. Councillors had three open briefings (60, 60 and 30 min). The approval was recorded as "CARRIED" with no division; the minutes' format does not record debate [S-B03 p.87] [S-B05]. Plan change: R28 records "no debate on record" as a fact, not a judgement.
  8. 21 Nov 2026 — Council documents from 2002 to 2025 (2024 gazettal, 2025 Urban Growth Management Strategy [UGMS]) treat the existing ring road as the freight route; SMEC (Aug 2026) still names it the preferred freight route while saying a northern bypass "should be explored". The October Vision map makes an outer freight ring road part of the Vision [S-B30] [S-B10 p.52] [S-B04 pp.50, 66, 74-75] [S-B03 p.92]. Plan change: new note Ring Road History.
  9. 21 Nov 2026 — Testing, modelling and costing of the Vision is scheduled for Feb–Jun 2027, after consultation and after Council endorses an "Ultimate Vision" in Feb 2027 [S-B03 p.78]. Plan change: our simulation could inform the Feb 2027 decision — a target date for results.
  10. 21 Nov 2026 — The one technical report (SMEC Phase 1, Aug 2026) has no one-way loop and no 30 km/h figure; it raises signals, lower central business district (CBD) speeds and a northern bypass only as untested options. The loop and a 30 km/h town centre first appear in Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) documents on the SMEC- and council-branded maps tabled 26 Oct 2026 [S-B04 pp.50, 52, 66, 70-71] [S-B03 pp.92-93]. Plan change: new notes Decision Timeline, Due Diligence Assessment.
  11. 21 Nov 2026 — The investigation was started by a councillor's Motion on Notice (Cr Schiller, 15 Dec 2025), carried in stronger words than the notice; SMEC was engaged in April 2026 [S-B07 p.15] [S-B03 p.77]. Plan change: decision graph built (resolution and person notes).
  12. 21 Nov 2026 — The 2015–16 council study said signals at Torrens/Seaview/Eyre would need three lanes per approach and land acquisition, and that the roundabout would hit capacity in summer peaks unless the Grantley Avenue rail crossing was closed [S-D21 s13.3.14–15]. Plan change: Grantley Ave closure added as candidate option N1; signal option C4 must model the land-take geometry.
  13. 21 Nov 2026 — Hindmarsh Rd volume grew ~38% (14,000 → 19,300/day, 2009–2024) while Torrens St stayed flat at ~15,500 [S-D02]. Plan change: demand model must reproduce growth on the approach, not the centre.
  14. 21 Nov 2026 — Town-centre peak hours are around midday (11:15–14:15), not commuter peaks [S-D19]. Plan change: Simulation conditions reworked: "weekday peak" means midday.
  15. 21 Nov 2026 — Crash hotspots are right-angle crashes at side-street priority junctions (Acraman/Hill, Acraman/Seaview, Armstrong/Crozier) and the Torrens/Eyre roundabout; one pedestrian fatality (Jan 2023) near Torrens/Stuart [S-D16]. Plan change: safety is a criterion alongside delay when judging options.
  16. 21 Nov 2026 — Torrens St and Hindmarsh Rd are Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) Primary Freight and Strategic Routes [S-D08]. DIT's Speed Limit Guideline (primary text, version 3; extracts) allows 30 km/h or 40 km/h in town centres and similar pedestrian areas, but says the high-pedestrian-activity limit is not intended for streets with short holiday or tourism peaks and that freight routes or major traffic routes are unsuitable for it [S-M04 s5.7, s5.7.1]. Correction: the first version of this entry, written from a secondary summary [S-D22], said the guideline uses 40 km/h for pedestrian areas; the primary text shows 30 km/h is allowed too. Plan change: new note Approvals and Constraints.
  17. 21 Nov 2026 — No intersection in town is signalised today; the proposed signals would replace the existing roundabouts at Torrens/Seaview/Hindmarsh and Torrens/Crozier [S-E01]. Plan change: C4 is simulated as roundabout → signals; R15 compares both.
  18. 21 Nov 2026 — Parking: average occupancy across the ~2,000 on-street spaces in the 2017 study area was 26–29% on a typical weekday [S-A08b p.11], but the core central business district (CBD) (Ocean St between Torrens and Coral St, Coral St between Torrens St and Flinders Pde) was consistently more than 80% occupied on a typical weekday (Wed 2 Dec 2015) as well as on 30 Dec 2015, when All streets within the CBD including the length of Flinders Parade were at or near capacity [S-D21 s7.2]. Correction (21 Nov 2026): an earlier version of this entry presented the area-wide average as if it applied to the core. Plan change: parking search is concentrated in the core blocks (Ocean St, Coral St); not modelled explicitly for now, recorded as an assumption.
  19. 21 Nov 2026 — Council recently made Almond Avenue one-way by public notice under s.32 of the Road Traffic Act 1961 (adopted 24 Nov 2025) [S-A07e]. Plan change: stream B to check what process a town-centre one-way loop needs.
  20. 21 Nov 2026 — Torrens Street / Hindmarsh Road is a State road (route B37) [S-A08a p.5] [S-E01]. Plan change: Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT) approval process added to questions; stream D checking.
  21. 21 Nov 2026 — A council-endorsed 2017 strategy (Tonkin Consulting) kept the Torrens/Victoria roundabout because signals would likely add delay (not a 2026 signal site), recommended a roundabout (since built) at Torrens/Crozier, and left signals at Hindmarsh/Seaview/Eyre open pending modelling [S-A08a p.14] [S-A08a p.13]. The Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) fact sheet publishes no data and no consultant [S-A03]. Plan change: new notes 2017 Town Centre Traffic Strategy and Questions for Council; R5 partially answered.
  22. 21 Nov 2026 — The consultation platform is Social Pinpoint (survey receipt sent from socialpinpoint.com; files on an hdp-au-prod-app-victor-yoursay-files S3 bucket) [S-H01]. Useful for finding the document library. Plan change: none.
  23. 21 Nov 2026 — Brent already submitted the survey on 20 Nov 2026 [S-H01]. Plan change: R24 becomes a written submission / email to council and councillors rather than a survey response.
  24. 21 Nov 2026 — Specifics from the survey wording: 30 km/h applies to all areas of the town centre; shared-use zones are 10–20 km/h; signals are at Torrens Street/Hindmarsh Road × Seaview Road and × Crozier Road [S-H01]. Plan change: C1, C2, C4 in the Options Register.
  25. 21 Nov 2026 — VHITS is the Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy, and it includes Ring Road options (new outer ring road vs retain current ring road) and a future growing north community [S-H01]. Plan change: new requirement R27 (ring road options in scope); streams A, B, C told to cover it.
