Investigation Plan

Master plan for testing Victor Harbor Council's proposed town-centre traffic changes against evidence and simulation. Every requirement has an id (R#) and a status. Every requirement traces back to Brent's words in the Plan Validation note, so nothing he asked for gets dropped. Expected findings are tracked in the Expected Information Catalogue; anything we didn't plan for goes to the Discoveries Log and, if it changes the plan, back in here as a new requirement.

Status key: todo · partial · done · blocked / not public

Time-critical

R24 Consultation feedback closes Sunday 5pm — confirmed 5:00pm Sunday 22 Nov 2026 [S-A01]. Brent has already done the survey; further input goes by email to localgov@victor.sa.gov.au. Anything Brent wants to submit has to be ready before then; the full simulation will not be. Plan a short evidence-based submission from whatever the research sweep finds by Sunday, and keep the deeper work going afterwards (council decisions come after consultation closes, so later findings can still go to councillors directly).

Scope

  • R17 In scope: changes to roads and traffic — circulation (one-way loop), intersections (signals, roundabouts), speed limits, lane configuration, shared-use zones (they change vehicle speeds and pedestrian priority, so they affect traffic). Out of scope: public transport options, recreational improvements, digital parking signage. Parking is only modelled where it causes traffic (cars circling for spaces).

Method requirements (apply throughout)

  • R18 Provenance for every fact: link to the primary source, archive a copy in sources/, record retrieval date and hash in the Sources Register. Rules: Research Conventions.
  • R19 Pre-plan what we expect to find (to spot misses) and log what we didn't expect (to widen the plan).
