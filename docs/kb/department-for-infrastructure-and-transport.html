Department for Infrastructure and Transport

South Australian Government department, the Department for Infrastructure and Transport (DIT), formerly the Department of Planning, Transport and Infrastructure (DPTI). Owns Torrens St, Hindmarsh Rd, Victoria St and other State roads; approves every speed-limit change; engaged by the Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) team in June 2026.
