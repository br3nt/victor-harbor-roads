OC3792025 Traffic Strategy Investigation Motion

15 Dec 2025 — Council, item 18.3 Motion on Notice. Made by the City of Victor Harbor.

Directed Administration to "commence a Traffic and Transport Investigation and Analysis Report" in 2025/26 as phase one, with phase two costed as a 2026/27 budget bid. The carried wording goes further than the notice, which sought to "Investigate and provide costings for the development of a comprehensive Traffic Strategy" (S-B06 p.318); the officers' narrower alternative (cost Urban Growth Management Strategy [UGMS] actions first) was not adopted. The minutes do not record how the wording changed. [S-B06 pp.318-321] [S-B07 pp.15-16]
