Vocabulary

Link types used in this knowledge base beyond the OKF base profile, so the decision graph is self-describing. Each is a rel on a link from the first note to the second; the target shows it as a backlink.

moved-by
The resolution was moved by the target person.
seconded-by
The resolution was seconded by the target person.
chaired-by
The meeting that made the decision was chaired by the target person.
decided-by
The target was a member present and entitled to vote when the decision was made (no division was called, so individual votes are not recorded unless stated).
absent
The target member gave an apology or was absent for the decision, as recorded in the minutes.
authored-by
The report, document or officer commentary was written by the target (person or organisation).
consulted
The target officer is listed as consulted on, or in attendance for, the report.
affiliation
The person held a position in the target organisation.
commissioned
The organisation engaged the target consultant for the work.
evidence
The decision relied on, or was accompanied by, the target document.

Base-profile terms also in use: chapter, describedby, related, glossary, tag, external.
