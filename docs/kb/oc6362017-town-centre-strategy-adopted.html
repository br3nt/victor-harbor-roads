OC6362017 Town Centre Strategy Adopted

23 Oct 2017 — Council (via Strategic Planning and Development Policy Committee). Made by the City of Victor Harbor.

On the recommendation of the Strategic Planning and Development Policy Committee (17 Oct 2017), adopted the Town Centre Traffic Movement and Car Parking Strategy Parts A and B and the Torrens Street review "as amended" (Grantley Avenue left-in/left-out trial). The strategy kept the Torrens/Victoria roundabout, recommended a roundabout at Torrens/Crozier, and proposed no one-way loop and no 30 km/h. [S-B25 pp.9-10] [S-B22]
