Sources: the council's Event Calendar 2026/27 (last updated 4 Nov 2026), which gives the dates, venues, whether roads are closed, set-up and pack-down periods, and expected attendance (published as "PAX") [S-M03]; the earlier council events page [S-C31]. Expected attendance counts people, not vehicles; the calendar does not say whether it is a total or a daily figure for the multi-day events. The calendar says whether road closures are needed but does not name the roads. Also seasonal but unquantified: whale season (May–October), Saturday farmers' market, Cockle Train, Granite Island visitors.

Inference: by the published attendance, the Rock N Roll Festival (12,000) is the biggest event of the year, followed by New Year's Eve (8,000), the Half Marathon (7,500) and the Rotary Art Show (7,000, spread over eight days). The New Year's Eve figure of 8,000 [S-M03] is 60% above the 5,000 our demand model first assumed (its event.attendees setting); that assumption is being raised to 8,000. We do not know how many of the attendees arrive by car.

What this means for the simulation

  • Normal: school-term weekday (midday peak) and Saturday.
  • Holiday peak: a weekday between Christmas and New Year — residents plus the full visitor layer.
  • Event: New Year's Eve (8,000 expected) or Schoolies (4,000) at Warland Reserve, with the published road closures [S-M03] — the hardest test for a one-way loop that routes all traffic past the foreshore (R23a).
