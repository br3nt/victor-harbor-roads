Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) Consultation Maps

Author: SMEC.

Nine draft maps (Why; Where is the growth; From the Urban Growth Management Strategy [UGMS]; Retaining the high-speed Ring Road; The Vision; Town Centre; Freight Route; Public Transport; Walking and Cycling) attached to the 26 Oct 2026 council report [S-B03 pp.88-96]. Every map carries the SMEC logo beside the council's. In Victor Harbor Integrated Transport Strategy (VHITS) documents, these maps are where the one-way loop and a 30 km/h town centre first appear; the "Vision" map states the town centre is converted to a low-speed environment (30 km/h) and a one-way loop is implemented to improve traffic flow, and shows an "Outer (Freight) Ring Road (indicative)" [S-B03 p.92]. The record does not show who originated each element. The report lists a "key staff" workshop on 30 Sep 2026 under consultation [S-B03 p.79]; inference: the maps were finalised after it. The consultation versions are on VHITS Preliminary Vision.
